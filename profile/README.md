[**Smart Second Brain**](https://github.com/s2b-dev/smart-second-brain) is an Obsidian plugin that
makes your vault smarter: better search, an interactive knowledge graph, and an AI assistant that
actually knows your notes. Search and the graph work right away with no AI provider — connecting
one unlocks the full agent. Desktop and mobile.

We build it in the open, in our spare time, because we use it every day.

## What we believe

**Your notes are yours.** We build on the [Obsidian manifesto](https://obsidian.md/about): files
on your disk, in plain markdown, that will outlive any app. Everything the plugin creates follows
the same rule — agents, skills, and memory are ordinary notes you can read and edit, and chats are
stored in an open format you can take with you. Nothing leaves your machine except what you
configure, and there is no telemetry.

**Everything in the open.** All development happens in public: the roadmap, the issues, the
discussions, the pull requests. No private fork, no held-back "pro" version.

**Free, and staying free.** We are not trying to make money from this. There is no paid tier, no
subscription, and nothing to upsell. AI can genuinely help with the way you think and write — and
it can just as easily get in the way. We would rather get that right for everyone than charge for
it.

**As few settings as possible.** The plugin is deliberately kept close to an MVP. Good defaults
beat a wall of toggles, so great UX should come out of the box. When something is genuinely
missing, the community proposes it and we decide together — that is what
[Discussions](https://github.com/s2b-dev/smart-second-brain/discussions) are for.

**Standards over lock-in.** Where an open format or an existing convention exists, we use it
rather than inventing our own.

It is not finished, and not everything is perfect yet. It is at the point where we are confident
enough to release and build on top of it in public.

## Get involved

The project is meant to be community-driven, and that only works if people actually show up.

- 💬 [**Discussions**](https://github.com/s2b-dev/smart-second-brain/discussions) — ask questions,
  propose ideas, or share a skill or agent you built
- 🐛 [**Issues**](https://github.com/s2b-dev/smart-second-brain/issues/new/choose) — report a bug
  or request a feature
- 🔧 [**CONTRIBUTING.md**](https://github.com/s2b-dev/smart-second-brain/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)
  — development setup, how to add a provider or a bundled skill, and how we handle AI-assisted
  contributions

You do not have to write TypeScript to make a real difference — skills and agents are just
markdown notes.
